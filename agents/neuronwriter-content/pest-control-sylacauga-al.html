Pests We Treat in Sylacauga

Subterranean Termites

Eastern subterranean termites are the main structural concern in Talladega County. They live in the soil, forage upward, and build mud tubes to bridge from soil to wood. They work out of sight, and the first sign is usually a spring swarm or damage found during a repair.

EnviroCare is a Sentricon Certified Specialist. We install Sentricon Always Active in-ground bait stations in the soil around the structure and monitor them on a schedule. Termites feed at the station and carry the bait back toward the colony, addressing the nest rather than only the foundation line. There is no slab drilling and no chemical trenching. Termite protection is quoted after a free on-site inspection. Qualifying homes may receive up to $1,000,000 in EnviroCare damage repair coverage, subject to inspection and the terms of the agreement.

Ants

Argentine ants and odorous house ants stay active through mild Talladega County winters and move indoors after heavy rain or during dry stretches. They are covered on the regular pest plan. Fire ants nest in open turf and mound after rain. Fire ant treatment is a separate whole-yard service: $150 covers most yards, larger properties are quoted by size, it carries a one-year warranty, and you do not need a pest plan to get it.

Carpenter ants show up on wooded Sylacauga lots, excavating galleries in damp or damaged fascia, trim, and framing. Where you find them, there is usually a moisture problem worth tracking down.

Spiders and Occasional Invaders

Garages, porches, stone and block foundations, and outbuildings are prime harborage for spiders, including widows. Crickets, millipedes, and ground beetles push toward structures when conditions shift. Exterior perimeter service is the main defense.

Rodents

Rodent work is part of the bi-monthly pest plan. Entry points are checked and sealed on exterior visits, and if rodents show up between scheduled visits, call the office and the team comes back out. Rodent removal is not offered as a standalone service.

Wasps, Hornets, and Carpenter Bees

Routine pest visits include treating eaves and webbing, which discourages wasps and hornets from building. Treating an active wasp or hornet nest is an add-on for pest customers at an additional charge. Carpenter bee treatment is available to existing EnviroCare customers only, as an add-on.

Mosquitoes and Ticks

Mosquito season runs March through October. Properties near Lake Howell, near creeks, and under heavy canopy carry the highest counts. Mosquito service reduces mosquito populations in the treated area by targeting resting sites and standing-water breeding habitat. It reduces the population - it does not eliminate mosquitoes. Ticks are a genuine concern on wooded lots and are treated alongside mosquito service. Both are priced separately from the base pest plan.

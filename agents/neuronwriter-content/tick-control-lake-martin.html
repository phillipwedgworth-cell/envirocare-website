Why Lake Martin properties see more ticks and pest pressure

Ticks gather where shade, humidity and animal traffic overlap, and lake properties have all three. Tree cover keeps the ground cool and damp. Deer and raccoons cross residential lots on their way to the water. Tall grass along fence lines, leaf litter against the foundation and wood stacked near the dock give ticks a place to wait for a host.

Weekend and vacation homes add another layer. When a Lake Martin home sits empty, wildlife has the yard to itself, and children and pets walking the grass on Saturday morning can pick up ticks before anyone checks. The species we see most in Tallapoosa County are the lone star tick, the American dog tick and the blacklegged tick, active from early spring well into fall.

How tick protection works for a Lake Martin home

Tick service focuses on where ticks actually live, not on open turf in full sun. The technician treats the edge between mowed lawn and woods, the wooded lots behind the house, dock and pier approaches, the path down to the water, fence lines and the areas around outbuildings. We use EPA-registered products according to label directions and keep appropriate distance from the water.

  • Walk the property and mark the shaded, brushy areas where ticks wait.
  • Treat those zones on a set schedule through the season.
  • Point out simple changes that help, like moving woodpiles and keeping grass short near paths, in line with EPA guidance on pest prevention and pesticide use.
  • Coordinate gate access, pets and the dock for homes that are not occupied full time.

Frequently asked questions from Lake Martin homeowners

Can pest control remove ticks from my yard?

Treatment reduces ticks in the areas you use most. No service can keep every tick off a property next to woods and water, so keep checking children and pets after time outdoors, and call if pest activity picks up before an infestation builds.

When should tick control start?

Tick activity picks up in early spring, so starting in March gives the program the best chance before your family is at the lake every weekend.

Is tick treatment safe near the water?

We use EPA-registered products according to label directions and keep the required distance from the shoreline. Tell the technician about pets, docks and swimming areas so precautions can be explained.

Can you service a weekend home when I am not there?

Often, yes, for outdoor work. Arrange gate access, pets and who should get the service notes before the visit. Interior service needs its own access arrangement.

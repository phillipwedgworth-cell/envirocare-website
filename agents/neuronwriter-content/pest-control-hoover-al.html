Common pests and pest problems every Hoover homeowner sees

  • Ants: odorous house ants and Argentine ants in kitchens, carpenter ants in the mature trees around Riverchase and Bluff Park.
  • Cockroaches: American roaches from crawl spaces and mulch beds, and the German cockroach that hitches a ride indoors and can turn into an infestation.
  • Spiders: black widows and other spiders in garages, storage areas and under decks.
  • Rodents: rats and mice, the rodent problem that starts when a mouse or rat looks for shelter when nights turn cool; technicians seal entry points they find during visits.
  • Mosquitoes and ticks: yards near Shades Creek, the Lake Cyrus greenway and wooded lots stay humid most of the year.
  • Termites: eastern subterranean termites are active in the clay soil throughout the Birmingham area.

Treating an active wasp or hornet nest, including yellow jackets that sting near doors, is an add-on for pest customers. Fire ants are a separate yard service. EnviroCare does not offer bed bug treatment or wildlife control.

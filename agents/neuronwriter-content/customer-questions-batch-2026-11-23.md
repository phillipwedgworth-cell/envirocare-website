### How can you tell carpenter ants from termite swarmers?
Winged ants generally have elbowed antennae, a narrow waist and unequal wing pairs. Termite swarmers generally have straighter antennae, a broader waist and similar-length wings. Identification can be difficult after wings are shed.
### Does finding winged insects mean I have structural damage?
No single sighting proves structural damage. Save a specimen or photograph if practical and arrange an inspection if you suspect termites or see recurring indoor swarmers. Do not rely on appearance alone to decide treatment.

How Do I Know Whether My Huntsville Home Has Termites?

Subterranean termite activity can be difficult to see. Possible warning signs include mud tubes along a foundation, termite swarmers, discarded wings and wood that appears damaged. Similar evidence can have other explanations, and visible signs do not reveal the full extent of an infestation. Request an inspection rather than relying on a photograph or a quick online diagnosis.

How Sentricon Termite Protection Works

Sentricon Always Active uses in-ground bait stations placed around the structure and serviced under an ongoing agreement. Station placement and the maintenance plan depend on a site inspection, available soil access, landscaping and other property conditions. The system is different from a soil-applied liquid termiticide. A technician can explain which approach EnviroCare offers and whether the property is suitable.

In-ground stations generally do not require drilling through a slab to install the stations. That does not mean every termite situation can be addressed without other investigation or treatment. Existing activity, inaccessible areas, construction details and changes to the property should be discussed before beginning service.

Termite Protection for New Construction and Existing Homes

A newly built home is not automatically free of termite risk. If the builder provided termite protection, request the written installation record, service provider information, renewal terms and any applicable warranty documents. Existing homes also need a property-specific review of accessible foundations, surrounding soil and evidence of activity. Do not assume the original treatment remains in force without checking its documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions About Huntsville Termites

Do subterranean termites only affect older houses?

No. Both new and established homes may be exposed. Foundation type and surrounding conditions affect what can be inspected and how protection is planned.

Can termite bait stations work around a slab foundation?

Sentricon stations may be suitable for many slab-foundation homes when the installation requirements and access conditions are met. A technician must assess the property.

How often are Sentricon stations inspected?

The monitoring and service schedule is determined by the applicable product instructions and your written agreement. Ask EnviroCare for the schedule that applies to your property.

Does general pest control include termite coverage?

No. Routine general pest control and termite protection have separate service scopes. Ask for both plans if you want them coordinated.

Is the $1 million figure a guarantee against all termite damage?

No. The amount is a potential contractual limit for qualifying homes. Eligibility, exclusions, claim procedures and continuing responsibilities are defined by the EnviroCare agreement.

Will the technician remove a Sentricon station if I change my landscaping?

Contact EnviroCare before disturbing stations or substantially changing grade, paving or landscaping around the foundation. The team can assess how the work affects station access and the agreement.

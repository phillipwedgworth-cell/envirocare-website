Why Bessemer Homes See Steady Pest Pressure

Three things drive the problem here. First, moisture. Valley Creek and its tributaries run through and around the city, and the low ground holds water long after a storm. Damp soil next to a foundation is the single most reliable predictor of subterranean termite and ant activity we see.

Second, age of construction. Large parts of Bessemer were built in the first half of the twentieth century, and those homes have settled. Mortar joints crack, sill plates sit closer to grade than modern code allows, and utility penetrations open up. Pests do not need a large opening - they need a continuous one.

Third, the interface between developed lots and woods. Properties on the Tannehill side back up to timber, and the pests living in that timber - carpenter ants, wasps, spiders, occasional rodents - treat the nearest structure as an extension of their range when weather turns.

Pests We Treat in Bessemer

Subterranean Termites

Subterranean termites can work out of sight. Swarmers, discarded wings, mud tubes or damaged wood are reasons to request a professional inspection. A photograph or a single sign does not establish the extent of activity or damage.

Our termite work uses Sentricon Always Active in-ground bait stations. EnviroCare is a Sentricon Certified Specialist. Stations are installed in the soil around the structure and monitored on a schedule. There is no slab drilling and no chemical trenching, which matters on older Bessemer brick and on homes where a drilled slab would mean tearing up finished floors. Termite protection is quoted after a free on-site inspection. Qualifying homes may receive up to $1,000,000 in EnviroCare damage repair coverage, subject to inspection and the terms of the agreement.

Ants

Argentine ants and odorous house ants persist through Alabama's mild winters and move indoors when it rains hard or turns dry. Nuisance ants are covered under the regular pest control plan. Fire ants nest in open turf and mound after rain. Fire ant treatment is a separate whole-yard service: $150 covers most yards, larger properties are quoted by size, it carries a one-year warranty, and you do not need a pest plan to get it.

Cockroaches

German cockroaches are an interior problem tied to harborage and food, common in older kitchens with dated cabinetry. American cockroaches come from sewer lines, drains, and mulch beds and push in through gaps at door sweeps and utility chases. The two need different handling, and part of the initial inspection is determining which one you actually have.

Rodents

Rodent work is part of the bi-monthly pest plan. Entry points are checked and sealed on exterior visits, and if rodents show up between scheduled visits, call the office and the team comes back out. Rodent removal is not offered as a standalone service.

Wasps, Hornets, and Carpenter Bees

Routine pest visits include treating eaves and webbing, which discourages wasps and hornets from building. Treating an active wasp or hornet nest is an add-on for pest customers at an additional charge. Carpenter bee treatment is available to existing EnviroCare customers only, as an add-on.

Mosquitoes and Ticks

Mosquito season in this part of Alabama runs March through October. Yards near Valley Creek, near the Bessemer Flea Market area, and anywhere with heavy shade and standing water hold the highest counts. Mosquito service reduces mosquito populations in the treated area by targeting resting sites and breeding habitat - it is a reduction program, not an elimination program. Mosquito service is available on its own or alongside a pest plan; Mosquito + Tick is a combined program.

Our approach is exterior-first. Most pests entering a home come from outside, so the treatment goes where they originate: the foundation line, eaves and soffits, window and door frames, utility penetrations, and the landscaping edges where mulch meets the house.

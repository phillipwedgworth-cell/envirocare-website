## How do I choose a reliable local exterminator?
Ask for appropriate Alabama licensing, a written description of covered pests, the treatment schedule, re-service conditions and any exclusions. For termite protection, compare monitoring, renewal obligations, retreatment and repair coverage rather than price alone.

## What pests are common around Alabaster homes?
Ants, cockroaches, spiders and termites are common concerns for Alabama homeowners. Moisture, gaps around doors and utility penetrations, and food sources can contribute to pest activity. A technician can identify the pest and recommend a treatment appropriate for the situation.

## What are three practical rules for preventing pests?
Remove food and water sources, reduce entry points, and monitor for activity. Repair leaks, store food securely and seal suitable gaps. Persistent problems may require professional identification and treatment.

## Are treatments appropriate around children and pets?
Tell your technician about children, pets, aquariums and any special concerns before service. Follow the product label and technician's preparation and re-entry instructions. Do not claim any pesticide is universally safe.

## Do I need to leave during pest control treatment?
That depends on the treatment, product label and treated location. Ask your technician before service; follow all preparation and re-entry directions.

## When should I call an exterminator instead of treating pests myself?
Consider professional help when pests repeatedly return, the source is unclear, activity is widespread or termite damage is suspected. Termite signs warrant an inspection rather than relying on general household insect sprays.

Mosquito control for Birmingham yards

EnviroCare Pest Services provides recurring seasonal mosquito-control treatments for Birmingham-area properties. A visit focuses on areas where adult mosquitoes rest and on practical ways to reduce standing-water breeding sites. Treatment can reduce mosquito activity, but rain, neighboring properties, and new breeding sites mean no company can responsibly promise complete elimination.

What should a homeowner compare?

Compare the actual treatment schedule, seasonal coverage dates, accessibility of outdoor areas, treatment limitations, and which additional pest services are included. Ask whether tick service is offered separately or as an add-on, and confirm the written price for your property. Do not assume a monthly payment equals a monthly application; billing and treatment frequency can be different.

Why might one treatment not solve the problem?

Mosquitoes can arrive from neighboring properties, and standing water can produce new adults after treatment. Regularly empty or refresh water in planters, toys, buckets, clogged gutters, and other containers where practical. These steps are useful alongside a professional treatment plan, but they cannot eliminate every mosquito in the neighborhood.

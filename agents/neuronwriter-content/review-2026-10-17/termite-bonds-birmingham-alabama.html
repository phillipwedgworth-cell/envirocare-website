What does a termite bond mean for an Alabama homeowner?

A termite bond or termite service agreement is a written contract explaining the inspection, treatment or monitoring program, ongoing obligations, renewal requirements and remedies if covered termite activity is found. Coverage varies by company and by agreement. Homeowners should review what triggers additional treatment, whether any damage repair is available, and the conditions or exclusions before purchasing.

How much does a termite bond cost in Birmingham?

There is no single dependable price for every property. Cost may depend on the size and accessibility of the structure, inspection findings, selected approach, existing termite evidence, and proposed monitoring and renewal terms. EnviroCare quotes termite protection after inspecting the property; a written proposal is more reliable than a statewide internet estimate.

Is Sentricon® repair coverage automatic?

No. Sentricon® is a termite baiting and monitoring system, not an insurer or provider of EnviroCare's repair promise. For qualifying agreements, after inspection and approval, EnviroCare may offer up to $1,000,000 in EnviroCare damage repair coverage, subject to the terms of the agreement. Request and read the actual agreement; the stated maximum is not a promise to pay every loss.

What should I ask before signing?

  • Which structure and areas are included in the agreement?
  • How often will stations be monitored or inspections performed?
  • What are my responsibilities to keep the agreement current?
  • What are the renewal terms, and what happens if I let it expire?
  • Does the offer include retreatment only, or conditional damage-repair coverage?
  • Who provides the repair coverage, and what exclusions and approval steps apply?

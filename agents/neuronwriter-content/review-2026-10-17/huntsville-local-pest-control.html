Choosing pest-control service for a North Alabama home

Ask what the recurring program actually covers, how often a technician visits, whether interior service requires a request, and what happens when pests return between scheduled visits. For EnviroCare, recurring household visits are primarily perimeter treatments; interior service is available by request. A written agreement should explain billing, scheduling, covered pests, and any re-service conditions.

Why inspect moisture and entry points?

Small exterior openings, stored materials near the foundation, poorly draining areas, and damp crawl spaces can make household pests more difficult to manage. The useful first step is to identify which pest is present and where activity is occurring. A treatment plan can then combine inspection, appropriate application, and practical exclusion recommendations instead of relying only on repeated product applications.

Is termite protection included with regular pest control?

Termite protection is a separate service. A termite inspection can identify relevant conditions and establish the scope of a Sentricon® monitoring or treatment proposal. Ask about the monitoring schedule, annual agreement, and what any offered EnviroCare damage repair coverage actually includes. The termite system itself does not provide the repair coverage.

What about mosquitoes around Huntsville homes?

Mosquito management focuses on reducing breeding opportunities such as standing water and providing treatment appropriate to the property during the offered season. Results depend on weather, breeding sites and surrounding conditions; no yard can responsibly be promised to remain mosquito-free.

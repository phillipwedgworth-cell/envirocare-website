What happens during recurring pest control?

Our recurring home pest-control service focuses on the exterior perimeter, where common household pests seek entry. Interior treatment is available when requested or when an inspection identifies a need. A technician can identify conditions that make re-entry more likely, such as gaps around doors, damp areas, and vegetation touching the house. The proposal and agreement identify the actual covered pests, schedule, and service terms.

Does an older Birmingham house require a different approach?

Not necessarily a different plan, but a careful inspection. Birmingham homes with crawl spaces, older masonry, mature landscaping, or additions may have more concealed entry points and moisture conditions. Ask the technician to look at foundation penetrations, utility openings, crawl-space moisture, and wood-to-soil contact. These observations guide exclusion and termite-protection recommendations; the age of a house alone does not establish an infestation.

What if ants return between visits?

First identify where ants are entering and whether moisture or exposed food is supporting the activity. Contact your local team about the current re-service terms; the technician can reassess the species and adjust the treatment approach rather than recommending repeated household spraying without identification.

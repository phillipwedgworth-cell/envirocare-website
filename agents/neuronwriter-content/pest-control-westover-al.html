Pest infestation risks on Westover properties

  • Fire ants: mounds in open turf and pasture edges, especially after rain.
  • Termites: eastern subterranean termites in crawl spaces, wood framing, porches and outbuildings where wood meets soil.
  • Mosquitoes: low-lying areas, drainage ditches and anything that holds water after a storm.
  • Rodents: mice moving from fields and woodpiles toward warm structures in fall and winter.
  • Household pests: a spider in the garage, a cockroach in the kitchen, ants, crickets and silverfish moving indoors as seasons change, before a small problem becomes an infestation.
  • Stinging insects: wasps building under eaves and inside sheds.

Residential Alabama pest control service

Our residential pest control plan covers more than 30 common Alabama pests on an every-other-month schedule, six visits a year. The technician focuses on the foundation, eaves, entry points and perimeter, with interior treatment as needed. This integrated pest management (IPM) approach pairs treatment with practical pest prevention steps, such as sealing gaps, moving firewood off the foundation and reducing moisture near the house, in line with EPA guidance on pest prevention and pesticide use. Re-service for covered pests between scheduled visits is at no extra charge. We use EPA-registered products according to label directions.

Pest control starts at $35 per month by ACH or $70 per every-other-month visit, with a $75 initial service (50% off the $150 regular price). Monthly pricing requires a 12-month service agreement, billed automatically by ACH auto-draft in equal, averaged monthly payments. Fire ants, mosquitoes, fleas and ticks are separate services, and treating an active wasp or hornet nest is an add-on. EnviroCare does not offer bed bug treatment or wildlife control.

Rodent control services for an Alabama home

Rodents are part of the residential pest program, because a rodent problem on a rural lot usually starts outdoors, and technicians seal entry points they find during visits: gaps around pipes, damaged vent screens, garage door corners and soffits. Additional trapping or exclusion work is scoped separately. Standalone rodent removal is not offered.

Termite control and Sentricon protection

EnviroCare is a Sentricon Certified Specialist. Sentricon uses in-ground bait stations around the home to target subterranean termite colonies, with ongoing monitoring and no drilling into the slab. New termite customers are offered Sentricon; liquid renewals and retreatments are for existing liquid customers. Termite pricing is confirmed after a free on-site inspection, and outbuildings can be inspected and quoted where appropriate.

The termite agreement includes up to $1,000,000 in EnviroCare damage repair coverage, subject to inspection and approval and subject to the terms of the agreement. A WDO letter for a real estate closing is a separate, paid service; confirm the current fee when you book.

  • Installation. Each bait station is set in the soil around the foundation, so installation is usually exterior work. A liquid barrier treatment often involves digging a trench along the foundation and, where a slab, patio or garage floor meets the house, may require drilling through it.

What happens inside the termite colony

Subterranean termites live in an underground colony and send worker termites out to forage for wood. When foragers find the bait in a station, they feed on it and carry it back to the colony, where it is shared with other termites. Noviflumuron is an insect growth regulator: termites that have eaten it cannot complete their next molt, so affected workers die when they try to molt. Because the effect depends on termites returning to feed, results build over weeks to months rather than days.

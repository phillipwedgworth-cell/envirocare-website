How to Read Birmingham's "Best Pest Control" Lists

Online directories can help homeowners discover pest-control providers, but they use different data, coverage areas and sorting methods. A directory's placement does not establish which company is right for a particular house. Review the listing's last-updated date, confirm the business location and read recent first-hand service reviews. A company that handles household ants may offer a separate agreement for subterranean termites; ask what is actually included.

Before choosing, request a written estimate that identifies the initial visit, recurring service frequency, covered pests, termite-monitoring requirements, cancellation and renewal terms. For a termite protection proposal, ask whether the written agreement includes retreatment, qualifying damage repairs or both. Confirm the inspection requirements and exclusions. Evaluate the contractor's Alabama licensing and whether the office serving you can provide the follow-up your property needs.

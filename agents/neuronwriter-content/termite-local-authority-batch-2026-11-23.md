H2: What are common warning signs of termites?
Suspected mud tubes, discarded wings, indoor swarmers and damaged wood can warrant a termite inspection. These signs are not a reliable measure of the extent of damage, and absence of visible signs does not establish that a home is free of termites.
H2: What should you do if you suspect termite activity?
Photograph the affected area if practical, avoid disturbing suspected activity and arrange an inspection. Do not rely on a household insect spray to address a suspected termite problem.

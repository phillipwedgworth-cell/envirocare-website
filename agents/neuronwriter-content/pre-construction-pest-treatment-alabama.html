What pre-construction termite treatment is

Pre-construction treatment is the protection installed during the building process rather than after a structure is finished. In Alabama's climate, subterranean termites are active nearly year-round, and they reach a building from the soil underneath and around it. Treating at the construction stage means the protection is in place before there is ever a finished floor, framing, or insulation in the way. For new builds, we install the Sentricon® Always Active system to establish ongoing colony protection around the structure from day one.

Why new construction in Alabama needs it

Alabama sits in one of the highest termite-pressure regions in the country. Warm temperatures, moisture, and clay soils give subterranean termites the conditions they thrive in. A new building is not immune simply because it is new — untreated soil beneath a fresh slab is an open invitation. Addressing it during construction is far less disruptive and far less expensive than tearing into finished work later. It also gives the eventual owner clean documentation, which matters at closing and for the warranty that follows the home.

What to Confirm Before Scheduling

  • Property and construction stage: provide the site address, foundation plans if available and current build status.
  • Access: identify planned paving, landscaping, drainage work and areas that may affect installation or later access.
  • Scope: confirm which structures and services are included in the written quote.
  • Timing: coordinate the proposed work with the project team. Appointment availability and construction changes must be confirmed.
  • Records: ask what treatment, installation or inspection documentation will be provided.
  • Handoff: establish who receives the service agreement, renewal information and contact details after the sale.

What Is Pre-Construction Pest Treatment in Alabama?

Pre-construction pest treatment is pest and termite protection planned around a new building's construction. The written proposal should identify the proposed termite control method, structures included, installation stage, access needs and ongoing service. For an EnviroCare project, ask the builder team to review those items before confirming the work.

Why Plan Termite Protection Before the Home Is Finished?

Construction schedules bring grading, utilities, concrete, paving and landscaping onto the same property. Planning ahead gives the builder and pest control company an opportunity to discuss how those activities affect access for the proposed termite treatment and later inspections. Share schedule changes with the service team so the agreed work can be reviewed before the site changes again.

Which Termite Treatment Options Should a Builder Compare?

Alabama Extension distinguishes soil-applied termiticides from termite bait systems. A soil treatment and an in-ground bait system have different installation and service requirements. For either option, ask how the method fits the foundation and finished site, how future access will be maintained and which continuing services the proposal includes. EnviroCare's builder consultation can address its Sentricon offering and the scope appropriate for the property.

How Long Does Pre-Construction Termite Protection Last?

There is no single duration that describes every method, property or service agreement. Ask about the proposed product, inspection schedule, renewal obligations and what happens if the site is disturbed by later construction or landscaping. For a Sentricon plan, the stations and ongoing service must be managed under the applicable instructions and agreement. Keep the renewal and contact information with the home's records.

Who Confirms Construction and Closing Requirements?

Ask the project team, local building official and closing professional which treatment records, inspections or documents apply to the particular project. Give the pest control provider those requirements before scheduling. A builder proposal should identify the documents that will be supplied and any separately quoted inspection or letter. Confirm these items in writing so the homeowner receives the correct records at handoff.

A Practical Builder Work-Order Checklist

  • Identify the site: include the lot or property address, builder contact and person coordinating appointments.
  • Describe the structure: provide the foundation type and identify detached buildings separately.
  • Confirm the proposed termite system: record the agreed method and installation scope.
  • Plan access: discuss paving, landscaping, fences and other work that could affect installation or later servicing.
  • Coordinate changes: establish whom to contact if construction dates or the site layout change.
  • List deliverables: identify installation records, any separately arranged WDO letter and the service agreement.
  • Prepare homeowner information: give the buyer the service contact, relevant records and the actual renewal and coverage terms.

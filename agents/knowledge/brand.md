## Strongest Differentiators (in order of impact)
1. **Sentricon® Certified Specialist** — $1,000,000 damage repair warranty from Corteva
2. **68-year track record** — founded 1958, predates most competitors
3. **Three generations of ownership** — same family, same values
4. **Lake Martin exclusivity** — ONLY pest control company with a dedicated Lake Martin office
5. **No contracts** — month-to-month, cancel anytime
6. **Local humans answer the phone** — no call center routing
7. **Same technician every visit** — customers know who is coming
8. **Fast local scheduling** — local routing and three Alabama offices mean quick turnaround, often within 24–48 hours (never promise same-day)

## Customer Profile
- Alabama homeowners, age 35–65
- Value local family businesses over national chains
- Trust built through familiarity and multi-year relationships
- Lake Martin customers: seasonal/vacation home owners, higher income, premium service expectations
- Huntsville customers: military/NASA families, new construction buyers
- Birmingham customers: established neighborhoods with older housing stock (termite risk)

## Brand Voice
- Warm, direct, Southern
- Never corporate-sounding
- Specific over vague ("68 years" not "decades of experience")
- Local references (Alabama weather, clay soil, lake country)
- Honest about what works and what doesn't (e.g., bug zappers don't work for mosquitoes)

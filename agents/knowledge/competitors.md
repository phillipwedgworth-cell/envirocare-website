# Competitor Intelligence

## Cook's Pest Control
- **URL**: https://www.cookspest.com
- **Founded**: 1928, Decatur AL (family-owned like us, but larger)
- **Markets**: Strong across Alabama, especially North Alabama / Huntsville
- **Review counts** (approximate): ~800+ Google reviews across locations
- **Strengths**:
  - Older brand recognition in Alabama
  - Strong Huntsville presence (their backyard)
  - Good digital presence and SEO
  - Responsive web design
- **Weaknesses**:
  - No Lake Martin office or dedicated lake service
  - Pricing is higher ($59+/mo starting price)
  - Less personal feel as they've grown
  - Corporate-style site, heavy on automation/scheduling
  - Call center, not always local technician
- **Steal this**: Online scheduling UX is clean; their "Get a Free Inspection" CTA is above the fold
- **Beat them on**: Lake Martin exclusivity, lower starting price, same technician promise, 1958 founding (we're 30 years newer than them but have better local story)

## Terminix
- **URL**: https://www.terminix.com
- **Alabama pages**: /branch-offices/alabama/, /branch-offices/alabama/huntsville/
- **Founded**: 1927, national franchise
- **Review counts**: High nationally, variable per location
- **Strengths**:
  - Massive brand awareness
  - Heavy Google Ads spend dominates "pest control near me"
  - Nationwide warranty/guarantee
  - Strong termite brand recognition
- **Weaknesses**:
  - National call center — customer gets a random technician
  - Contracts typically required
  - High churn due to impersonal service
  - Pricing is opaque (no prices on website)
  - Reviews often mention "technician changed every time"
  - Slow response time on non-emergency calls
- **Steal this**: Their "termite guarantee" messaging is clear and prominent
- **Beat them on**: Price transparency (we show prices, they don't), no contracts, local human answers, $1M Sentricon warranty vs their own warranty

## Orkin
- **URL**: https://www.orkin.com
- **Alabama pages**: /alabama/, /local/al/huntsville/, /local/al/birmingham/
- **Founded**: 1901, Atlanta-based, now Rollins Inc. (public company)
- **Review counts**: High nationally
- **Strengths**:
  - Enormous SEO budget and domain authority
  - Ranks #1 for most Alabama pest control keywords
  - Strong content marketing (lots of educational blog posts)
  - Clean, modern website design
- **Weaknesses**:
  - Contract-heavy, cancellation fees
  - Premium pricing without premium local feel
  - No Alabama-specific story or roots
  - Reviews consistently mention "couldn't reach local office"
  - No Lake Martin presence
- **Steal this**: Their pest library / educational content drives organic traffic; very detailed pest ID pages
- **Beat them on**: Family story, 68-year Alabama history, no contracts, local ownership, Lake Martin

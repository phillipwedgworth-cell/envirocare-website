# EnviroCare search improvement process

Updated November 9, 2026 (Central). This sequence supersedes the July feature
backlog in this standing document. It changes no schedule, subscription or
public account setting. Use the existing stack.

## Outcome and evidence

Grow qualified organic calls, quote requests and booked work. Google Search
Console measures organic search; Maps visibility, AI recommendations and
content scores are separate signals. None proves that an inquiry or job occurred.

Keep the settled 28-day baseline and preceding 28 days from the November 9 audit
in the private project record. Keep raw queries, leads and private account exports
outside this public repository. Record sources, dates, aggregation and gaps.

## Stage 1: correct verified problems

Prepare one small release:

- /quote: make metadata, heading and share cards describe a quote request and
  office callback, rather than a free treatment visit. Preserve the form,
  endpoint, routing and canonical.
- /blog/pest-control-cost-alabama: shorten the repetitive displayed heading;
  preserve the URL, search title and educational page role.
- /blog/how-to-get-rid-of-ants-in-house-alabama: remove unsupported success
  statistics, fixed treatment timetables, diagnosis from appearance alone and
  improvised product advice. Preserve the search title, URL, useful guidance,
  citations and service links.

Release gate: inspect the diff and rendered pages, build the app, run repository
guards and record any unavailable check. Check metadata, one H1, canonical,
JSON-LD, service terms and quote behavior. Obtain explicit production approval
as required by root AGENTS.md. A draft PR or passing build is not a release or
a ranking improvement.

## Stage 2: collect the missing Google evidence

Before changing the main pest or Huntsville search titles, collect native GSC
evidence for /services/pest-control, /huntsville, /birmingham, /pricing, the
indoor-ant guide and /quote:

1. URL Inspection: verdict, Google-selected canonical, last crawl and exclusion
   reason. HTTP 200 and index/follow tags do not prove indexing.
2. Performance: query-to-page pairs for matched settled current/prior windows,
   with device and country context. Separate query and page tables do not prove
   which URL Google selected for a query.
3. Page experience: the applicable mobile Core Web Vitals field report and
   actual call/form checks. HTTP fetch timing is not a field vital.
4. Conversions: verify quote-success and phone-click events, then reconcile
   them with qualified inquiries. A phone-link click is not a completed call
   or booked job. Do not submit test leads to the live office.

Use the existing authenticated account. Record an unavailable connection as a
gap; do not invent results or buy a replacement subscription.

## Stage 3: improve two priority pages at a time

| Priority | Page / relationship | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Main pest service and Huntsville | Use selected queries and URLs to target the specific intent, verified local evidence and contextual links that need work. Both already have descriptive titles and substantial content. |
| Next | Indoor-ant guide and commercial service | Inspect actual search-result intent and snippets. Keep useful homeowner/buyer answers and supported quote paths. Do not add an emergency service to chase a query. |
| Next | Pricing / cost guide; Birmingham / selection guide; termite / Sentricon | Confirm distinct transactional and educational roles. Consolidate only after query-page evidence supports it; preserve working URLs and redirects. |
| Protect | Sentricon value, green-cap and spider articles | Preserve successful search titles, URLs and useful content. Make necessary factual or customer-experience corrections only. |

Record the exact changes and real release time. For an editorial experiment,
avoid changing its title, body, design and links together. Accuracy corrections
can be grouped when necessary; record that confounding. A before/after
comparison is not causal proof, and an unchanged page is not a randomized
control. Keep a clear rollback commit.

## Stage 4: strengthen genuine local corroboration

Verify each office identity before correcting an obsolete citation. Preserve
Birmingham and Alabaster address/phone pairs and separate review histories.
Prioritize sources actually observed in relevant AI reports.

Prepare a consistent request for honest feedback through the existing Fieldster
process. Invite eligible customers consistently; do not filter for positive
experiences, offer rewards, invent reviews or supply the customer's wording.
Use the correct office link. No customer messages or public profile changes
are sent or activated by this document.

Use genuine approved local work, photos and service evidence. No fake office,
generated technician photograph, bought link or unsupported service claim.

## Stage 5: measure and decide the next release

Compare the first complete settled 28-day window after the actual production
release with the saved baseline. Review weekly direction without treating a
few low-volume clicks or incomplete recent days as a reliable trend.

Report these separately:

- Property clicks/impressions/CTR from property totals. Distinguish brand and
  customer-login navigation where disclosed query data permits.
- Target-page clicks and relevant non-brand queries. State query-data coverage
  and geographic limitations. Do not sum page impressions as property totals
  or add overlapping weekly exports.
- Qualified organic inquiries and booked work from verified conversion records.
- Maps and AI visibility from matching prompt/platform/grid/office cohorts.

Set numeric business-growth targets after the qualified-inquiry baseline is
verified. More impressions without relevant visits or inquiries is not success.
An accidental canonical/noindex change, broken call/quote path or unsupported
offer is a reason to stop and repair the release promptly.

Choose the next release from evidence. Do not restart a large article batch to
use credits, merge pages solely because their topics overlap, or repeatedly
change a winner while waiting for Google to recrawl it.

References: Google Search Central title-link and recrawl guidance; Google
Business Profile review guidance; UC IPM Ants and EPA product-use guidance.

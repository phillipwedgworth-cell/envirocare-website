## Our Position

Three Alabama offices: Birmingham/Alabaster · Alexander City/Lake Martin · Huntsville.
Tagline: *"No One Cares Like EnviroCare."*

We are NOT a franchise. We are NOT a call center. Every customer talks to a local technician who knows their neighborhood.

---

## Competitors We Must Beat

### Cook's Pest Control (Primary threat — Alabama native)
- Also family-owned, founded 1928, Decatur AL
- Strong in North Alabama, Huntsville is their backyard
- Weakness: corporate feel now, less personal since growth
- Weakness: no Lake Martin presence
- Weakness: pricing is higher at monthly plan level (~$59+/mo)
- Their site: heavy on "schedule online" automation, light on local stories
- **Beat them on**: Lake Martin exclusivity, personal family story, Sentricon $1M warranty

### Terminix (National chain)
- High brand awareness, hurts us on "pest control near me" searches
- Weakness: national call center, no local knowledge, high churn
- Weakness: pricing is opaque, contracts required
- Weakness: technician turnover — customer never sees the same face twice
- **Beat them on**: local relationships, same technician, real phone answered by humans

### Orkin (National chain)
- Strong SEO, high ad spend, ranks for almost every pest keyword in Alabama
- Weakness: same problems as Terminix — impersonal, contract-heavy
- Weakness: premium pricing without premium feel
- **Beat them on**: price transparency (we show pricing on site), family story, 1958 founding

### Wayne's Pest Control (Regional)
- Aggressive SEO investment in Birmingham market
- Weakness: newer company, no legacy
- **Beat them on**: 68-year track record, multi-generational trust, Sentricon certification

- "Family-owned since 1958"

- "Alabama Licensed & Insured"

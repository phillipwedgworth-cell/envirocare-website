# EnviroCare Website — Deploy in 10 Minutes

## What you have
A complete Next.js website. All pages built. All photos included.
Just needs to go from your computer to the internet.

---

## Step 1: Create GitHub account (2 min)
1. Go to github.com
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Use phillipwedgworth@gmail.com
4. Choose free plan

---

## Step 2: Upload this project to GitHub (3 min)
1. Once logged in, click the green "New" button
2. Name it: envirocare-website
3. Make it Private
4. Click "Create repository"
5. On the next screen, click "uploading an existing file"
6. Drag and drop ALL files from this folder into the browser
7. Click "Commit changes"

---

## Step 3: Deploy to Vercel (3 min)
1. Go to vercel.com
2. Click "Sign up" → Continue with GitHub
3. Click "Add New Project"
4. Select envirocare-website from your GitHub repos
5. Click Deploy (default settings are correct)
6. Wait 2 minutes

## Step 4: Your site is live!
Vercel gives you a URL like: envirocare-website.vercel.app
Test all pages work, then connect your real domain.

---

## Connect your real domain (envirocarellc.com)
1. In Vercel dashboard → Your project → Settings → Domains
2. Add: envirocarellc.com
3. Vercel shows you DNS records to add
4. Go to wherever your domain is registered (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
5. Add those DNS records
6. Wait up to 48 hours for DNS to propagate
7. Site is live at envirocarellc.com

---

## Every time you update the site
You don't need to know code. Just:
1. Work with Claude to update/add pages
2. Download updated files
3. Replace files in GitHub
4. Vercel auto-deploys in 2 minutes
